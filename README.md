## laboratory Overview

This repository contains the sequence analysis and documentation for a Cell and Molecular Biology Laboratory activity investigating how a DNA mutation can affect a protein product and contribute to a disease phenotype.

This focuses on the **HEXA** gene and the documented **c.1274_1277dup** variant associated with Tay-Sachs disease.

## Reference Sequences

The WT HEXA coding sequence was obtained from the NCBI RefSeq transcript **NM_000520.6**.

**Reference protein:** NP_000511.2

The WT CDS is **1,590 bp** and produces a predicted HEXA protein of **529 amino acids**.

## Documented Mutation

The documented HEXA mutation used in this laboratory activity is c.1274_1277dup. It duplicates four nucleotides, TATC, in the HEXA coding sequence. This causes a frameshift and produces a premature stop codon.

## Artificial Mutation

An artificial three-nucleotide deletion was also created in the HEXA coding sequence. Three nucleotides, GGC, were deleted. Since three nucleotides were removed, the reading frame was expected to remain unchanged.

## Main Results

The documented **c.1274_1277dup** mutation caused a frameshift and produced a premature stop codon. The WT protein was **529 amino acids**, while the predicted documented mutant protein was **431 amino acids** long.
